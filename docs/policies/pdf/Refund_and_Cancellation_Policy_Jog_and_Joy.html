1. POLICY OVERVIEW & CUSTOMER COMMITMENT

1.1. At Jog & Joy®, we design and manufacture high-performance kids activewear and everyday athleisure crafted with premium cottons, breathable spandex blends, and durable stitching. We want every child to feel joyful, confident, and comfortable in our garments.

1.2. We understand that shopping for kids clothing online requires flexibility, particularly regarding sizing, fit, and rapid childhood growth. This Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy is formulated in accordance with the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 to ensure a transparent, fair, and hassle-free post-purchase experience.

2. ORDER CANCELLATION POLICY

A. Cancellation by Customer (Prior to Warehouse Dispatch):

  • Eligibility & Timeline: You can cancel your order free of cost at any time before the parcel has been assigned an Air Waybill (AWB) tracking number and dispatched from our Ahmedabad fulfillment facility (usually within 12 to 24 hours of placing the order).
  • How to Cancel:

1. Access your customer account on the website and select "Cancel Order"; or

2. Call or WhatsApp our customer care helpline at +91 94299 05118; or

3. Send an email with your Order ID (e.g., #JJ-94821) to jogjoy85@gmail.com with the subject line "Order Cancellation Request".

  • Refund on Pre-Dispatch Cancellation: Upon receiving your pre-dispatch cancellation request, a 100% full refund (including any shipping fees paid) will be initiated immediately to your original payment method.

B. Cancellation After Dispatch (In-Transit Orders):

  • Once your package has been handed over to our logistics courier partner and is in transit, the order cannot be intercepted or cancelled digitally in our internal system.
  • Doorstep Refusal: You may simply decline delivery when the courier delivery executive arrives at your doorstep by stating "Order Cancelled". Once the parcel is returned to our warehouse by the courier (Return to Origin - RTO), your refund will be processed promptly.

C. Cancellation by the Company:

The Company reserves the right to cancel orders under exceptional operational circumstances, including:

  • Inadvertent inventory stock-outs, fabric lot rejections, or manufacturing defects identified during pre-dispatch quality checks.
  • Unserviceable delivery pin codes or logistics carrier strikes.
  • Identification of suspected fraudulent transactions, card verification failures, or unconfirmed Cash on Delivery (COD) phone numbers.

In all such instances, the customer will be notified immediately via SMS/Email, and 100% of the charged payment will be refunded.

3. 7-CALENDAR-DAY RETURN AND SIZE EXCHANGE WINDOW

3.1. Return Window: Customers are eligible to initiate a return or exchange request within seven (7) calendar days from the date of confirmed package delivery as recorded in the courier partner's tracking system.

3.2. Hassle-Free Size Exchanges: If a garment is slightly loose or snug for your child, we provide quick, seamless size exchanges. Subject to inventory availability, we will arrange a doorstep exchange or dispatch the desired size immediately upon pickup of the original item.

3.3. Mandatory Conditions for Return / Exchange Eligibility:

To qualify for a full refund or exchange, returned items must satisfy our standard hygiene and quality standards:

  • The garment must be completely unused, unworn, unwashed, and undamaged.
  • The item must be in its original brand packaging with all original fabric tags, barcode labels, size stickers, and care cards intact and attached.
  • The product must be free from household odors, perfume, detergent fragrance, pet hair, dirt, makeup marks, or signs of wear.
  • Complimentary gifts, accessories, or free items received as part of a bundle or promotional deal must be returned along with the main item.

4. NON-RETURNABLE AND NON-EXCHANGEABLE ITEMS

For reasons of personal hygiene and child health safety, certain product categories cannot be returned or exchanged once the packaging seal is broken:

  • Undergarments, innerwear, briefs, and bloomers.
  • Socks, stockings, and hair accessories.
  • Custom-printed, personalized, or altered garments.
  • Products explicitly marked as "Final Clearance" or "Non-Returnable" on the product details page.
  • Items returned past the 7-calendar-day return window.

5. DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE, OR INCORRECT PRODUCTS RECEIVED

5.1. Zero Customer Liability: While every garment undergoes strict 3-tier quality inspection prior to packaging, if you receive a product that is visibly damaged, stained, has a stitching defect, or is not the item/size you ordered:

  • Notification Timeline: Please notify our support desk within 48 hours of package delivery.
  • Required Proof: Send a brief description along with 1 to 2 clear photographs of the defect and the package shipping label via WhatsApp (+91 94299 05118) or Email (jogjoy85@gmail.com).
  • Resolution: We will immediately arrange a priority reverse courier pickup at 100% zero cost to you and simultaneously dispatch a brand-new replacement parcel, or issue a 100% full refund at your preference.

6. REVERSE PICKUP LOGISTICS PROCEDURE

6.1. Doorstep Reverse Pickup: We arrange doorstep reverse pickup across over 19,000+ pin codes through reputed logistics carriers (Delhivery, BlueDart, Xpressbees).

6.2. Courier Pickup Attempts: The courier partner will make up to three (3) pickup attempts at your delivery address. Please ensure the garment is securely packed in its original bag or a clean tamper-evident envelope.

6.3. Self-Shipment in Non-Serviceable Pin Codes: In the rare event that your pincode is not serviceable for reverse courier pickup by our logistics network, our team will request you to self-ship the item via India Post Speed Post or a local courier to our Ahmedabad warehouse. We will reimburse your verified courier shipping expenses up to ₹100 upon receipt of the postal receipt.

7. QUALITY INSPECTION (RQC) AT WAREHOUSE

All returned items arriving at our Ahmedabad fulfillment center undergo a physical Return Quality Check (RQC) within 24 to 48 business hours of delivery:

  • If the item passes quality inspection (unworn, original tags present, unwashed), your refund or size exchange will be approved immediately.
  • If an item fails RQC due to visible signs of use, washing, missing tags, or customer damage, our support team will contact you with photo evidence. Items failing RQC will be returned back to the customer's address.
