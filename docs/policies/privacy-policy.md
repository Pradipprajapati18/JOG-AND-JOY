## 2. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL DATA COLLECTED
We collect information only to the extent necessary to provide you with seamless shopping, accurate parcel deliveries, and customer care services. The data collected falls into the following categories:

### A. Personal Identification Data (Provided Directly by You):
- **Full Legal Name:** Required for shipping documentation and invoicing.
- **Contact Details:** Primary mobile telephone number, WhatsApp contact, and valid email address.
- **Delivery and Billing Addresses:** Complete residential/office postal address, apartment/flat number, landmark, city, state, postal pin code, and country.
- **Customer Account Credentials:** Encrypted passwords, profile preferences, saved shipping addresses, and size selection history.

### B. Transactional & Order Information:
- Order identifiers, purchase history, items added to bag or wishlist, garment sizes, color variants, SKU codes, quantities, and gross/net invoice totals.
- Chosen payment method (e.g., UPI, Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking, or Cash on Delivery).

### C. Financial & Payment Information (Processed via Secure Gateways):
- All online payment transactions are processed through **Razorpay Software Private Limited** (RBI-authorized Payment Aggregator).
- **CRITICAL DATA ASSURANCE:** Jog & Joy **DOES NOT** collect, store, view, or retain your complete 16-digit debit/credit card numbers, CVV security codes, card expiry dates, net banking passwords, or UPI MPINs on our internal databases or cloud servers. All sensitive financial authentication occurs within Razorpay’s encrypted, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateway.

### D. Technical, Device and Log Information (Collected Automatically):
- Internet Protocol (IP) address, device manufacturer, operating system version, browser type and version.
- Timestamps of visits, page browsing velocity, clickstream patterns, referring websites, and interaction metrics.

## 3. LEGAL BASIS AND PURPOSES OF DATA PROCESSING
We process your personal data exclusively for lawful and specified business purposes:
- **Contract Fulfillment:** To verify orders, generate tax invoices, package goods, and coordinate doorstep delivery via licensed courier carriers.
- **Real-Time Communication:** Transmitting automated order confirmations, dispatch alerts, courier AWB tracking links, and delivery notifications via SMS, WhatsApp, and email.
- **Customer Support & Returns:** Authenticating return/exchange requests, coordinating reverse pickup couriers, resolving inquiries, and processing refunds under our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
- **Payment Verification & Anti-Fraud Security:** Identifying and preventing fraudulent transactions, chargeback abuses, bot traffic, and unauthorized access to user accounts.
- **Legal & Tax Compliance:** Maintaining statutory sales records and tax invoices as mandated under the Central Goods and Services Tax Act (CGST), 2017 and Indian commercial codes.
- **Platform Optimization:** Analyzing aggregate navigational data to enhance website responsiveness, optimize layout rendering, and improve product availability.

4.1. **Use of Cookies:** We utilize cookies, local storage items, and session identifiers to facilitate essential website functions, such as keeping you authenticated, preserving items in your shopping cart across sessions, and recording size selector preferences.

4.2. **Types of Cookies Deployed:**
- **Strictly Necessary Cookies:** Essential for page navigation, session persistence, and secure checkout processing.
- **Performance & Analytics Cookies:** Collect anonymized metrics to evaluate page loading speed and visitor traffic distribution.

## 5. DATA SHARING AND DISCLOSURES TO THIRD PARTIES
We enforce a strict **Zero Data Monetization Policy**. We **never** sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal information or contact details to third-party telemarketers or external advertising networks. Your personal data is shared solely with trusted service providers necessary for business operations:

1. **Logistics & Courier Carriers:**
   - Name, delivery destination address, and phone number are shared with verified logistics partners (including **Delhivery, BlueDart, Xpressbees, DTDC, Shiprocket**, and **India Post**) exclusively to enable the courier agent to locate your address and deliver your package.
2. **RBI-Regulated Payment Aggregators:**
   - Transaction values, order references, customer name, email, and mobile number are transmitted over 256-bit SSL encrypted channels to **Razorpay** to facilitate online payment authorization.
3. **Communication Gateway Providers:**
   - Mobile numbers and email addresses are securely processed by authorized transactional SMS and email delivery partners to dispatch one-time passwords (OTPs), invoice PDFs, and delivery status alerts.
4. **Law Enforcement and Legal Authorities:**
   - We may disclose personal data if required to do so by applicable law, search warrants, court orders, or governmental directives, or when necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of the Company, our customers, or the public.

## 6. DATA RETENTION AND ARCHIVAL PROCEDURES
6.1. **Retention Period:** We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including order processing, warranty tracking, customer service, and satisfying statutory requirements.

6.2. **Statutory Tax Retention:** Tax invoices, customer billing records, and transaction histories are archived for a mandatory minimum period of **eight (8) financial years** in strict compliance with the **Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017** and the **Companies Act, 2013**.

6.3. **Account Deletion:** Upon receiving a verifiable account deletion request from a registered customer, all non-statutory personal data, profile tokens, and marketing associations will be permanently purged from our active operational databases within **48 hours**.

## 7. USER RIGHTS UNDER INDIAN DATA PROTECTION LAWS
In accordance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** and applicable Indian data protection frameworks, you are entitled to the following rights:
- **Right to Access & Information:** You may request a complete summary of the personal data we hold regarding your account and identity.
- **Right to Correction & Updating:** You have the right to correct inaccurate, obsolete, or incomplete personal data (e.g. updating delivery address or phone number) through your customer account settings or by emailing our support desk.
- **Right to Erasure / Deletion:** You may request the deletion of your account and personal data, subject to mandatory tax and financial retention laws.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Where data processing is based on voluntary consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time without impacting prior lawful processing.
- **Right of Grievance Redressal:** You have the right to register complaints regarding your personal data with our Grievance Officer and receive time-bound resolution.

## 8. MINORS AND CHILDREN'S PRIVACY PROTECTION
8.1. **Protection of Minor Data:** While **Jog & Joy®** specializes in athletic apparel and casual clothing designed for children and teenagers, our online shopping platform is intended exclusively for access and commercial use by adults aged 18 and older.

8.2. **Parental Consent:** We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under the age of 18 without verifiable parental or guardian authorization. All orders for children's activewear must be placed by a parent, guardian, or adult custodian. If we discover that personal data of a minor has been collected without parental consent, we will take immediate measures to purge such records from our servers.

## 9. TECHNICAL DATA SECURITY SAFEGUARDS
9.1. **Encryption Standards:** All data exchanged between your browser and our storefront servers is secured using industry-standard **Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure (HTTPS)** with **Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / 256-bit SSL encryption)**.

9.2. **Server Security & Access Controls:** Our database systems are maintained in enterprise cloud environments featuring multi-tier firewalls, automated vulnerability scanning, strict role-based access controls (RBAC), and multi-factor authentication for administrative staff.

9.3. **Security Incident Protocol:** While we implement rigorous technical precautions, no electronic transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed 100% impenetrable. In the unlikely event of a security incident affecting personal data, we will follow statutory reporting protocols and notify affected users in accordance with applicable CERT-In and DPDP Act guidelines.
