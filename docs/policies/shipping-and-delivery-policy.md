## 7. DOORSTEP DELIVERY ATTEMPTS AND RTO PROTOCOL
7.1. **Delivery Attempts:** The courier delivery executive will attempt delivery at your designated address up to **three (3) consecutive times** on consecutive business days.
7.2. **Customer Contact:** Prior to delivery, the courier executive will typically place a phone call or send an OTP SMS to your registered mobile number to confirm availability.
7.3. **Non-Delivery & Return to Origin (RTO):** If delivery fails after three attempts due to:
- Customer unreachable or phone switched off;
- Door locked or incorrect/incomplete delivery address; or
- Refusal to accept the parcel without prior cancellation notice;
the parcel will be designated as **Return to Origin (RTO)** and transported back to our Ahmedabad warehouse. For prepaid orders, the order will be cancelled upon warehouse receipt and refunded back to your source account minus applicable forward-and-reverse freight deductions.

## 8. DAMAGED, LOST, OR TRANSIT-TAMPERED PARCELS
8.1. **Tamper-Evident Packaging:** All Jog & Joy orders are sealed in branded, heavy-duty, tamper-evident security polybags or corrugated boxes with reinforced tape.

8.2. **Inspection at Delivery:** If you notice that the shipping parcel is open, visibly damaged, retaped, or tampered with at the time of delivery:
- **Do NOT accept delivery**, or immediately instruct the courier executive to record *"Delivered in Damaged Condition"* on their electronic delivery sheet;
- Take 1 to 2 clear photographs of the outer package and shipping label; and
- Immediately notify our customer care team at **+91 94299 05118** or **jogjoy85@gmail.com** within **24 hours**.

8.3. **Zero Loss Guarantee:** Once verified, the Company will immediately dispatch a brand-new replacement parcel at zero additional cost, or issue a 100% full refund. The customer bears zero financial liability for courier transit damage or lost shipments.
